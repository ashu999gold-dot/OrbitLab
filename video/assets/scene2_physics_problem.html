The Classical Two-Body Problem

Mutual Newtonian Gravitation & Center-of-Mass Coordinates
ME 206 · Statics & Dynamics
Primary Body m₁
Secondary Body m₂
Barycenter (COM)
Gravitational Forces F₁₂, F₂₁
1. Newton's Universal Law of Gravitation
F = G · (m₁ · m₂) / r²
Mutual attractive gravitational force acts along the line connecting centers of mass, decaying inversely with the square of distance.
2. Action-Reaction & Momentum Invariance
F₁₂ = -F₂₁ ⇒ Σ F_ext = 0
Collinear equal-and-opposite forces satisfy Newton's 3rd Law. Total linear momentum is strictly conserved across all time: P_total = const.
3. Center-of-Mass (Barycentric) Motion
R_CM = (m₁r₁ + m₂r₂) / (m₁ + m₂) = 0
Neither body remains stationary. Both bodies execute Keplerian trajectories around their common barycenter, which serves as the inertial frame.
